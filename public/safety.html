Safety Standards

Last updated: September 2026

1. Provider Verification

Every provider on Handled completes a verification process before their listing goes live. This includes identity verification against a government-issued document, and a phone number check. Providers who offer specialised services (such as electrical work or gas fitting) may be required to show trade certification before listing those specific service categories.

2. Booking Best Practices

Always book through the Handled platform so your booking record, agreed price, and provider assignment are stored in your account. Bookings made outside the platform are not covered by our review and dispute system. When meeting a provider for the first time, schedule the appointment during daylight hours where possible and let a family member know the time and expected duration.

3. Reporting Concerns

If a provider behaves in a way that feels unsafe, threatening, or unprofessional, report it immediately using the contact details below. Our team will investigate and take appropriate action, which may include removing the provider from the platform and notifying relevant authorities where appropriate. All reports are treated confidentially.

4. Cancellation & Refunds

You can cancel a pending or accepted booking free of charge up to 12 hours before the scheduled start time. After that window, cancellation may still be possible but the provider may choose to charge for the lost slot. Handled mediates disputes but does not process refunds directly; payment is settled between customer and provider.

To report a concern or ask about a specific case, contact safety@handled.demo.
