Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who We Are

A service marketplace for digital, creative, home, business, and personal work. Browse providers, request a service, and get it handled. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect on the platform, how we use it, and the choices you have over your data.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the name, email address, and phone number you provide. When you book a service, we store the booking details (service title, provider name, preferred date and time, and any notes you add for the provider). Reviews you publish are stored against your account so other users can verify authenticity.

Providers may also publish a public listing with their bio, specialty, pricing display, and response time. This information is visible to anyone browsing the directory.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to facilitate bookings between you and providers, to send service-related notifications, to display your activity history on your account page, and to detect abuse, such as fake reviews or spam bookings. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Your Rights

You can edit your profile information at any time from the My Profile page. You can request deletion of your account by contacting support. Reviews you publish remain visible to other users for transparency, even after the originating booking is no longer active.

For questions about this policy, contact privacy@handled.demo.
